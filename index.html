What I do

Most SME financing requests are not declined because the business is weak. They are declined because the file does not answer the questions a credit committee is obliged to ask. I close that gap before submission.

Credit file preparation
A complete financing package assembled to bank underwriting and credit committee standards: business and sector context, risk assessment, facility rationale, and the supporting analysis a reviewer expects to find without asking for it.
Financial statement rebuilding
Incomplete or poorly presented accounts reconstructed into structured statements and cash flow projections. Where the numbers exist but are not in a form a lender can assess, this is usually the whole problem.
Facility structuring
Advice on facility type, tenor, and repayment structure measured against your cash conversion cycle and debt service capacity — so the request you make is one a lender can reasonably approve.
Pre-submission review
An honest read of a file before it goes out, identifying the weaknesses that most commonly cause SME applications to fail in committee, and what can be remedied first.
