Why good businesses get declined

  • The statements don't tell the story

    Figures are incomplete, inconsistent, or laid out in a way a credit analyst can't assess quickly.

  • There's no clear cash flow picture

    The bank can't see how the loan will be repaid from the business's own income.

  • The request doesn't fit the business

    The amount, term or type of financing doesn't match how cash actually moves through the company.

Common questions

Can you guarantee my loan will be approved?

No. The lender makes the final decision, and anyone who promises approval isn't being honest with you. What I can do is make sure your file is complete, clear, and gives your business its best fair chance.

Will you change my numbers to make the business look better?

No. Every file is built from your real figures. The work is presenting them accurately and clearly. That is what lenders trust, and it is what protects you.

What do I need to prepare?

Everything needed to prepare your financial statements. Typically that means your commercial register and tax card, financial statements or accounting records for the last two to three years, six to twelve months of bank statements, and details of any existing loans. If some records are missing, tell me and we will work out what is possible.

How long does it take?

Usually one to two weeks from the moment your documents are complete. The more complete your files are at the start, the faster it goes.

How are fees charged?

A fixed fee for each finished file, agreed before any work begins. We both sign a contract in Arabic and English that sets out exactly what is included. Conversations before that are always free.

Which lenders and sectors do you work with?

All of them. The work centres on your financial statements, so it applies whether you are approaching a bank, a leasing company or a non-bank lender, and whatever your sector or business size.

Do you work with businesses outside Cairo?

Yes. I work with clients across Egypt, online or at your premises.
